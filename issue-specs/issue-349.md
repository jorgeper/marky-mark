# Spec: shadows (#349)

## Goal

All acceptance criteria in issue-specs/issue-349.md are satisfied for issue #349, with evidence visible in the session: the toolbar's bottom shadow (B) has the blur and spread the page's shadow has today (`--mm-panel-shadow`'s 24px blur / 2px spread, cast downward) while the page proper's shadow (A) is much tighter and fainter (blur at most 12px, no spread, alpha at most 0.09) through a page-specific token, with floating panels, the comment-nav pill and the file tabs keeping a consistent look; the page's top-right corner reads rounded like the top-left with the scrollbar present, and the page's shadow shows on the ground right of the page next to the comments column instead of being cut off by it; a new e2e test (E602) pins these end states; `npm run validate:quick` has been run once in the implementer's session and passes; and a summary comment from the implementer exists on issue #349.

## Acceptance criteria

The issue's screenshot marks two shadows: **A** is the page's shadow (the white document surface, `.workspace-stack > .workspace`, today `--mm-panel-shadow: 0 0 24px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.14)`), **B** is the top pane's shadow (the toolbar band, `.toolbar`, today `--mm-toolbar-shadow: 0 2px 10px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`). The owner's request, read in order: B takes the spread A has today; A then becomes much tighter and fainter; and the page's top-right corner should look like the top-left, rounded, with the shadow diffusing around it rather than stopping sharply.

### A. Shadow B (toolbar) takes the page's current spread

- The toolbar's computed `box-shadow` has the same blur radius and spread radius as `--mm-panel-shadow` (24px and 2px, which stays unchanged as the floating-panel token) and a downward y-offset of at least 2px, so it reads as the top pane casting onto the page and ground below. Its alpha is at least today's 0.08 and at most `--mm-panel-shadow`'s 0.14. Observable: parse `getComputedStyle(document.querySelector('.toolbar')).boxShadow` (Chromium serialises `rgba(r, g, b, a) x y blur spread`) and compare blur/spread with the `--mm-panel-shadow` probe value.
- The shadow is visible in both toolbar modes: the static bar (default, `.theme-root.toolbar-static`) and the auto-hide overlay bar (SPEC4 §2.4; the existing shell-and-menus test that asserts the returning toolbar's shadow is not `none` still passes).
- The comment navigator pill (`.comment-nav`) does NOT grow with it: its computed `box-shadow` is unchanged from today (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08) 0px 2px 10px 0px`), via a token of its own on `.theme-root` (a literal shadow may live only in a token definition — the style lint enforces this).

### B. Shadow A (page) is much tighter and fainter

- The page proper's computed `box-shadow` is not `none`, has a blur radius of at most 12px, a spread of 0px and an alpha of at most 0.09 (today: 24px / 2px / 0.14). It resolves through a page-specific internal token on `.theme-root` (suggested name `--mm-page-shadow`, declared beside `--mm-toolbar-shadow` with a comment citing issue #349); `--mm-panel-shadow` itself keeps its value and use (menus, popovers, dialogs, the workspace card and every other floating-panel consumer keep their look).
- The file tabs stay "the page's shadow at tab scale": `--mm-tab-shadow` is re-derived from the new page shadow (the same alpha, blur no larger than the page's, no spread) and its comment in `src/styles.css` says so; E353's "tab shadow equals the token value" assertion keeps passing and no tab's shadow is `none`.
- Print output is unchanged (the `@media print` rules still flatten the page: `box-shadow: none`).

### C. The page's top-right corner is rounded and its shadow is not cut off

- With a document tall enough to scroll (the vertical scrollbar of the page's scroller present at the page's right edge, with the default auto-hiding scrollbars on), the page's top-right corner reads rounded exactly like the top-left: no scrollbar track, gutter fill or thumb paints a square corner over the rounded arc, and the ground colour shows outside the arc at the top-right as it does at the top-left. Observable end state: a pixel sample just outside the top-right arc (e.g. 1px in from the page's right edge, 1px below its top edge) is the ground colour (`--mm-bg-elevated` of `.body-row`), not the page's fill and not a scrollbar colour, and the page's computed `border-top-right-radius` still equals `--mm-radius-small`. Either the scroller's track starts below the corner (a `::-webkit-scrollbar-track` margin or similar) or the rounded sheet clips the scroller; whichever mechanism, the page proper (`.workspace-stack > .workspace`) remains the element that carries the radius, outline and shadow (E353, E589, W19 and the hosted sheet test key on it) and remains the scroller the auto-hide fade targets.
- The page's shadow shows on the ground to the RIGHT of the page when the comments column is open, symmetric with the left side next to the sidebar: the comments pane (`.comments-pane`, positioned and later in DOM order, so today it paints its `--mm-bg-elevated` fill over the page's shadow) no longer occludes it, and the sidebar does not either. Observable: with the comments column open, the pixel 3px right of the page's right edge at mid-height is darker than the pane's flat ground colour by at least as much as the pixel 3px left of the page's left edge is darker than the sidebar's ground (or, if the implementer prefers a DOM-derivable proxy, the page column paints above both pane wrappers — e.g. `.workspace-stack` has an integer computed `z-index` ≥ 1 while the panes' is `auto`). Whatever mechanism is chosen, modal dialogs, the app menu, comment popovers, the toolbar shell and the edge-cluster pills still paint above the page and the panes (the existing e2e suites cover these; E579, E583, E595 and the shell-and-menus tests pass unmodified).
- The band above the corner stays flat (issue #340): the strip band, the column and the pane wrappers still have `box-shadow: none`.

## Context

Tokens live on `.theme-root` in `src/styles.css`: `--mm-panel-shadow` (line ~77, chrome scale, shared by every floating panel), `--mm-toolbar-shadow` (line ~147, internal, used by `.toolbar` ~561 and `.comment-nav` ~1646) and `--mm-tab-shadow` (~163). The page proper is `.workspace-stack > .workspace` (~2221: fill, top radius, four-sided outline, shadow; it is also the `overflow: auto` scroller). `.toolbar-shell` is absolutely positioned at z-index 80, so B's larger shadow already paints over the page and the panes; A's right-side occlusion comes from `.comments-pane` (~953, `position: relative`, later sibling) painting its ground over the earlier positioned `.workspace-stack` — `z-index: 1` on the stack is the likely fix, but check that nothing inside the stack needs to rise above out-of-stack chrome. Auto-hiding scrollbars (default on, `.theme-root.autohide-scrollbars`, ~3575) reserve an 11px gutter on `.workspace` / `.split-preview` / `.cm-scroller`; find which rule paints the square corner before fixing it. The style lint (`scripts/style-lint.mjs`) rejects literal `box-shadow` values outside token definitions. Interpretation note: "B the same spread as A" is read as B taking A's pre-change geometry (the issue points at B being faint and A being wide), then A shrinking — not both matching the new A.
