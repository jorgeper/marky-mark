# Spec: Highlights: cards and lifecycle (activate, recolor, note-less behavior) (#232)

## Goal

All acceptance criteria in issue-specs/issue-232.md are satisfied for issue #232, with evidence visible in the session: clicking any highlight activates its card offering four recolor swatches, note add/edit, and remove — recoloring updates the entry's `color`, repaints its marks, and re-arms the last-used color; noted highlights keep the full existing lifecycle (replies, resolve with ghost tint, delete) via `CommentCard.tsx`; note-less highlights show no standing card in the margin panel — their card appears only while active and leaves on deactivation — while noted highlights keep today's always-present panel cards and comment-nav behavior; `npm run validate:quick` passes in the implementer's session; and a summary comment from the implementer exists on issue #232.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Activation reaches every highlight (PRD 022 Req 8).** Clicking a painted range (`mark.hl` via `handleMarkClick` in `src/App.tsx`) or a panel card activates that highlight's card, for noted and note-less entries alike, on both comment surfaces (full preview and split-edit live preview). Existing activation feel is kept: card scrolled into view, mark centered/flashed on card click (SPEC14 §1.3), click-away deactivates (SPEC14 §3.1).
- **The active card offers recolor (Req 8).** The active card shows the four marker swatches (order/vocabulary `MARKER_COLORS` in `src/lib/anchoring.ts`: yellow, green, blue, pink). Clicking one sets the entry's `color`, repaints its marks immediately (the `data-color` ride on `mark.hl`), persists through the existing update path, and updates the `lastMarkerColor` `U`-setting (Req 4 — "creating or recoloring with a swatch updates it", per #231). Recoloring a legacy colorless entry gives it a `color` (stamping then follows the lowest-version rule from #230); entries the user didn't touch stay colorless.
- **The active card offers note add/edit and remove (Req 8).** A note-less highlight's card offers an "add note" affordance that opens note editing; submitting a note turns it into a noted highlight (body set, standing card from then on). Noted highlights keep root-note edit. Every card offers remove/delete, which deletes the entry and unpaints its marks (existing `deleteComment` path; the thread-delete confirmation may stay for noted entries).
- **Copy-link is out of scope but not blocked.** The copy-link affordance the card hosts is Req 10, owned by the links sub-issue (#233-family) — do not implement it here; just don't structure the card so it can't be added.
- **Noted lifecycle unchanged (Req 8).** Noted highlights keep the full existing lifecycle — replies, edit, resolve (ghost tint per SPEC6 §3, including "Show resolved"), reopen, delete — via `src/components/CommentCard.tsx`. Per PRD 022 Req 6, reply and resolve remain available only once a note exists: a note-less highlight's card offers neither.
- **Note-less highlights don't clutter the panel (Req 9).** A note-less entry (empty `body`, no thread) produces no standing card in the margin panel flow; its card exists only while it is active and leaves when deactivated (click-away, activating another highlight, etc.). Noted highlights keep today's always-present panel cards, panel ordering, and comment-nav behavior (SPEC14 navigator/hotkeys must not land on phantom cards for inactive note-less entries — either skip note-less entries or activate them properly; pick one and state it in the summary).
- **No regressions in adjacent flows.** The #231 flows still hold: swatch-create (no composer), "add note" composer attachment and its cancel-undo, type-to-comment, orphaned badge, read-only (`readOnly`/`mayComment`) withholding of authoring controls — the recolor/remove/add-note affordances are authoring controls and must be withheld the same way.
- **Tests.** Unit tests for any new pure logic (e.g. a standing-card/lifecycle predicate, if extracted). E2e in `tests/e2e/comments.spec.ts` (continuing the `E<n>` numbering after E418) covering at least: recolor from the active card repaints and persists; a note-less highlight shows no standing card, shows a card while active, and the card leaves on deactivation; adding a note to a note-less highlight yields a standing noted card; note-less card offers no reply/resolve. Existing comment e2e suites pass with only mechanical updates.
- **Test economy.** The implementer iterated with `npm run typecheck` and `npm run test:unit` (or tests targeted at the changed code, e.g. `npx playwright test -g '<title>'` for one e2e), and ran the full quick gate exactly once, right before declaring the goal met — not after every small change, and not as a baseline at the start of the attempt.
- **Gate.** `npm run validate:quick` has been run in the implementer's session and prints `QUICK VALIDATION: ALL PASSED`.
- **Issue trail.** A summary comment from the implementer exists on issue #232.
