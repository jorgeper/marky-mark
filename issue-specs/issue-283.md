# Spec: Comment format 2.0.0: kind-split records, marker color vocabulary, annotation tint tokens (#283)

## Goal

All acceptance criteria in issue-specs/issue-283.md are satisfied for issue #283, with evidence visible in the session: the comment format is at 2.0.0 with every record carrying an explicit `kind` (`"comment"` — `body`/`thread`/`resolved`, no color; `"highlight"` — `color`, no body/thread/resolved) and `docs/COMMENT-FORMAT.md` documenting it with a changelog entry; the highlight color vocabulary is exactly yellow/green/orange/pink with `blue` dropped at parse and the marker tint tokens gaining orange and losing blue; comments render in one fixed blue tint from theme-overridable tokens in the document-rendering region of `src/styles.css`; a pre-2.0.0 or malformed store opens with no annotations, no error dialog and no crash while new annotations still save as a 2.0.0 store; and `npm run validate:quick` passes.

## Acceptance criteria

### Format 2.0.0 — the record shape

- `SUPPORTED_COMMENT_FORMAT_VERSION` in `src/lib/commentFormat.ts` is `2.0.0`, and `BASELINE_COMMENT_FORMAT_VERSION` is the lowest version that can represent a 2.0.0 record (`2.0.0`, since `kind` is required on every record) — each declared as its own literal, not derived from the other, as the existing comments there insist.
- Every stored record carries a required `kind` field whose value is exactly `"comment"` or `"highlight"`. A record with a missing, non-string or unrecognized `kind` is dropped at parse (skipped whole, like today's schema-failing entries) — never crashes the parse, never guessed at.
- Comment records carry `body`, `thread` and `resolved` and no `color`. Highlight records carry `color` and no `body`, `thread` or `resolved`. The in-memory type in `src/lib/anchoring.ts` expresses the split (e.g. a discriminated union on `kind`) so that reading `body` off a highlight, or `color` off a comment, is a type error rather than a runtime `undefined`.
- The per-kind known-key sets (today's single `COMMENT_KEYS`) and the fixed emission order for each kind are defined in `commentFormat.ts`, and the treatment of a foreign known-key (e.g. `color` on a comment record, `body` on a highlight record) is decided explicitly, documented in `docs/COMMENT-FORMAT.md`, and pinned by a unit test — whichever rule is chosen, it is stated rather than left to fall out of the unknown-key bag by accident.
- The PRD 004 machinery is retained, not rewritten: the anchor schema is unchanged; unknown keys are still bagged per object into `extra`/`extraVersion` and re-emitted at the level they were read from; `stampedFormatVersion` still stamps the lowest version capable of representing the data actually present (with each new/changed field carrying its own Min version); serialization is byte-stable and idempotent (serializing the same set twice produces identical bytes); zero annotations still means no container at all.
- Frozen-store behaviour for a **newer** MAJOR is retained: a store declaring `3.x` (or an uninterpretable version) is unreadable, freezes authoring, and shows the existing persistent `store-unreadable` indication — `readSidecar`/`splitEmbedded` keep reporting `readable: false` for that case.
- `docs/COMMENT-FORMAT.md` is updated end to end for 2.0.0: the "Current format version" line, the payload/comment/reply/anchor tables (with per-kind tables and Min-version columns), the worked example, the reader and writer rules, and a **newest-first `### 2.0.0`** changelog entry that states the kind split, the color-vocabulary change, and the deliberate break with `1.x` readers/stores.

### Colors and tint tokens

- The highlight color vocabulary is exactly `yellow`, `green`, `orange`, `pink` — in `COMMENT_COLORS` (`commentFormat.ts`), `CommentColor` / `MARKER_COLORS` (`anchoring.ts`), and the `lastMarkerColor` settings validator in `src/lib/settings.ts` (a persisted `blue` fails validation and falls back to the default, rather than surviving as a fifth color).
- `blue` is invalid at parse: a highlight record whose `color` is `"blue"` (or any other non-vocabulary value) is dropped, not coerced to a default tint and not crashed. This is a deliberate change from 1.1.0's "invalid color reads as absent" and is stated in the changelog.
- The marker tint tokens gain `--mm-marker-orange` and drop `--mm-marker-blue` everywhere they appear: `src/styles.css` (token block, the `mark.hl[data-color=…]` hue rules, the `.marker-swatch-*` faces), `editor/default-theme.css`, `editor/styles.css`, `editor/THEMING.md`, `THEMES.md`, and the `themes/gruvbox-dark.css` overrides. No `--mm-marker-blue` reference survives (`grep -rn 'mm-marker-blue'` returns nothing outside `archive/`).
- The orange hue meets WCAG AA contrast (≥ 4.5:1) for body text over the painted mark in the bundled themes — at minimum the default `.theme-root` hue at its idle (42%) and active (60%) strengths, and the `gruvbox-dark` override, as PRD 022 Req 13 required. The computed contrast ratios are shown in the implementer's session (a throwaway script is fine) and reported in the issue comment.
- Comments render in one fixed **blue** tint defined as theme-overridable `--mm-*` tokens inside a `DOCUMENT RENDERING — BEGIN/END (PRD 018 Req 17)` region of `src/styles.css`, distinct from the four `--mm-marker-*` hues and never aliased to one of them. The equivalent editor-surface rule in `editor/styles.css` uses the same token contract, and the token(s) are listed in `editor/THEMING.md` and `THEMES.md` like their marker neighbours.

### Legacy and malformed stores

- Opening a document whose sidecar or trailer predates 2.0.0 (a `1.x` version string, the integer `1`, or no version key at all) shows **no annotations and no error dialog or persistent notice**: the `store-unreadable` indication is absent for that case, authoring is not frozen, and saving new annotations writes a 2.0.0 store over it. (Legacy annotations are intentionally lost — PRD 023 non-goal "no migration of 1.x comment stores".)
- Malformed input never crashes: non-object payloads, non-array `comments`, entries that are not objects, wrong-typed fields, and entries missing required per-kind fields are skipped and the app opens the document normally.
- `fixtures/interop-sidecar.comments.json` and any test that loads it are updated coherently — either regenerated as a valid 2.0.0 store or repurposed as the legacy-tolerance fixture — so no suite silently asserts a 1.x store still parses.
- `tests/frozen/alpha4-reader.ts` is **not** edited to track `src/` (its header forbids it). The MAJOR bump deliberately breaks its PRD 004 Req 39 forward-compatibility assertion; the test that uses it is updated to assert the intended break (an alpha.4 reader no longer round-trips today's output), with a comment naming issue #283 and the changelog entry saying so.

### Existing surfaces keep working

- This slice ships **no new authoring UX**: the PRD 023 interaction changes (killing the popup, Marky Mark menu entries, hotkeys, the comments pane — Reqs 6–20) are explicitly out of scope. The existing surfaces are adapted to the kind-split records and keep working: the selection popup / edit-mode affordance (now offering the four colors with orange in blue's place), the comment panel cards and navigator, editor-side painting via `mapHighlightsToSource`, and copy-link.
- Creating a highlight writes a `kind: "highlight"` record; "add note" / type-to-comment writes a `kind: "comment"` record. The `hasNote` standing-card predicate and every other "is this a comment or a highlight" test in `src/App.tsx` / `src/components/CommentCard.tsx` is re-expressed against `kind` rather than against an empty body. Where an existing affordance has no meaning under the split (e.g. a card's recolor swatch row for a record that is now always a comment), it is removed or reduced, with a citation comment naming this issue.
- Behaviour changed or added in this slice carries a citation comment in the repo's format (`PRD 023 §<req>` / `SPEC<n> §x.y`, per `.sandcastle/CODING_STANDARDS.md`), and `docs/MAP.md` is regenerated with `npm run map` if any citation moved (the validation gate diffs it).

### Verification

- Unit coverage lands with the slice in `tests/unit/comment-format.test.ts` (and `tests/unit/anchoring.test.ts` where the types moved) for: parse and serialize round-trips of both kinds, byte-stable key order per kind, the `kind` rules (missing/unknown kind dropped; foreign per-kind fields handled as documented), `blue` rejection, the version stamping and newer-MAJOR frozen behaviour, and legacy/malformed store tolerance.
- Existing unit and e2e suites are **updated** to the new record shape and color vocabulary rather than deleted — `tests/e2e/comments.spec.ts` in particular still exercises swatch insert, recolor, persistence and painting, against orange where it used blue.
- The implementer iterated with `npm run typecheck` and `npm run test:unit` (or vitest targeted at the changed files); the full gate was run **once**, right before declaring the goal met — not after every change and not as a start-of-attempt baseline (baseline with the quick tier only).
- `npm run validate:quick` has been run in the implementer's session and passes, printing `QUICK VALIDATION: ALL PASSED`.
- A summary comment from the implementer exists on issue #283, stating what changed, the WCAG AA numbers for the orange (and blue comment) tints, and the `validate:quick` result.

## Context

The format seam is `src/lib/commentFormat.ts` (version rules, key sets, parse/serialize, stamping) with the in-memory types and the color vocabulary in `src/lib/anchoring.ts`; the two containers that read and write through it are `src/lib/sidecar.ts` and `src/lib/embedded.ts`, and neither should learn about `kind` beyond what the seam gives it. The published contract is `docs/COMMENT-FORMAT.md` (620 lines; the "Current format version" header, the schema tables with their Min-version columns, reader/writer rules, and the newest-first changelog all need the 2.0.0 pass); `CONTRIBUTING.md` makes that changelog a review gate.

Marker tints live in the `DOCUMENT RENDERING — BEGIN/END (PRD 018 Req 17)` region of `src/styles.css` around lines 750–890 (token block, `mark.hl[data-color]` hue rules, `.marker-swatch-*` faces), mirrored in `editor/styles.css` + `editor/default-theme.css` with the contract table in `editor/THEMING.md` and the theme-author table in `THEMES.md`; `themes/gruvbox-dark.css` is the one bundled theme that overrides the four hues today. Read `docs/STYLE-GUIDE.md` before touching chrome, and `editor/AGENTS.md` before touching the package.

App-side consumers are `src/App.tsx` (`createHighlight`, `recolorHighlight`, the marker popup buttons, the SPEC25 carry, `pending`/type-to-comment, the frozen-store notice around line 7715 and the load path around line 1555 that decides `readable: false`) and `src/components/CommentCard.tsx`. `src/lib/settings.ts` validates `lastMarkerColor` against `MARKER_COLORS`.

Background: `prd/023-comments-highlights-split.md` Reqs 1–4 are this slice (Req 5's overlap-as-independent-layers and Reqs 6–21 belong to later slices); parent issue #276 has the owner's framing. Marky Mark is unreleased, so breaking the format is intended — but a legacy store must degrade to "no annotations", never to a crash or a scary dialog.
