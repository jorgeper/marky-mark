# Spec: scrollbars for comments (#339)

## Goal

All acceptance criteria in issue-specs/issue-339.md are satisfied for issue #339, with evidence visible in the session: with the comments pane open, the pane's scroll box (and so its vertical scrollbar) begins at the same vertical point as the document's scroll box — the top edge of the page proper below the tab strip band, within 1px, in every document mode, with the strip shown or hidden and in static-toolbar mode; the band-high region above the pane is plain `--mm-bg-elevated` ground with no bar running through it; the pane still hugs the page's right edge at its fixed 300px, still scrolls its own card flow to the end, and cards stay level with their anchors; a new desktop e2e test proves the alignment and `npm run validate:quick` passes in the implementer's session; a summary comment from the implementer exists on issue #339.

## Acceptance criteria

### A. The two scrollbars start on the same line (the issue's one ask)

- With the file tab strip shown and the comments pane open, the top edge of the pane's scroll box equals the top edge of the page proper, within 1px. Observable: the bounding-box top of `[data-testid="comments-pane"]` equals the bounding-box top of `.workspace-stack > .workspace` (the element issue #340 gave the page's outline, radius and shadow), and equals the bounding-box bottom of `[data-testid="file-tab-strip"]`, each within 1px. Today the pane's top is the body row's top, a full `--mm-tabstrip-h` (38px) higher than the page's — that is the offset in the issue's screenshot.
- This holds in every document mode: full preview, plain edit, and split edit (where the document scrollers are the CodeMirror scroller and the split preview inside the page; the page's top edge is still the reference).
- The pane's scrollbar is the scrollbar of that scroll box: it runs from the page's top edge down to the body row's bottom and never extends up into the band region. Observable: `[data-testid="comments-pane"]` is the element with `overflow-y: auto` (as today) and no ancestor between it and `.body-row` scrolls; its height equals the body row's height minus the band's height, within 1px.
- The strip-high region above the pane (the wrapper's top `--mm-tabstrip-h`) is ground: the same computed `background-color` as `.body-row` and `.file-tab-strip` (`--mm-bg-elevated`), with no `box-shadow`, no border and no radius painted on it or on the pane's top edge. PRD 025 Req 6 (amended by issue #340) still holds: the band, the sidebar, the comments column and the ground above the page corner are one flat plane.
- The strip hidden (`settings-file-tabs` off, `.body-row` without `with-tabs`): there is no band, so the pane's scroll box top equals the page's top equals the body row's top, within 1px. The spacer exists only while the strip does; nothing keys on the strip's absence with a hardcoded height.
- Static-toolbar mode (`.theme-root.toolbar-static`, the in-app toolbar with auto-hide off): the pane clears the overlaid toolbar exactly as the strip does today (`.theme-root.toolbar-static .workspace-stack.with-tabs .file-tab-strip { margin-top: 42px }`), so the equality with the page's top edge holds there too, with and without the strip. The current `.theme-root.toolbar-static .comments-pane { padding-top: 42px }` clearance moves out of the scroll box (onto whatever carries the band spacer) so the bar does not run under the toolbar; the 42px figure is not duplicated as a third literal — reuse the strip's rule or one shared declaration.
- Any band height change follows automatically: the spacer reads `--mm-tabstrip-h` (the token at `src/styles.css`, `.theme-root { --mm-tabstrip-h: 38px }`), never a literal 38.

## Context

The issue body is one sentence and a screenshot: the comments pane's scrollbar starts higher than the document's; "align their starts vertically". No comment thread.

Why it happens: `.body-row` (`src/styles.css:2001`) lays out three flex children stretched to its height — `.folder-wrap`, `.workspace-stack` and `.comments-wrap`. The stack is a column whose first child is `.file-tab-strip` (`--mm-tabstrip-h`, 38px) and whose second is `.workspace`, the page proper and document scroller (issue #340 put the page's outline, radius and shadow on it; `src/styles.css:2171`). `.comments-wrap` (`:915`) and the `.comments-pane` scroll box inside it (`:927`, `overflow-y: auto`) span the whole body row, so the pane's bar starts 38px above the page's top. Markup: `src/App.tsx:8852` (`comments-wrap` → `aside.comments-pane` → `panelInner`, the `.panel` card flow at `:7720`). The folder sidebar already lands its list below a 38px header sized to the strip ("38px, the tab strip's height" at `.folder-header`, `:2440`), so the sidebar's own scroll box begins at the page's top — the precedent the pane should match.

The natural mechanism: the wrapper (or the pane as a column with a non-scrolling band-high spacer above the scroll box) takes `--mm-tabstrip-h` of top space when `.body-row.with-tabs` (the class `src/App.tsx:8331` derives from `showFileTabs`), plus the 42px toolbar clearance under `.theme-root.toolbar-static` the way `.file-tab-strip` takes it (`:2410`); the scroll box itself starts at the page's top. Keep the scroll box as `[data-testid="comments-pane"]` with `ref={commentsPaneRef}`: `layoutFlowCards` (`src/App.tsx:7039`) reads its `scrollTop`, the pane-scroll listener (`:7117`) filters on it, and `scrollIntoView` (`:4820`, `:7611`) scrolls inside it.

Scrollbar look is issue #167's domain (`src/lib/autoHideScrollbars.ts`, `SCROLLBAR_FADE_SELECTOR`, the `.autohide-scrollbars` block at `src/styles.css:3522`); the pane is deliberately not in that list today and this issue does not add it.

Tests to know: `tests/e2e/file-tabs.spec.ts` E583 (cluster geometry via `.comments-wrap` and `.workspace-stack` rects), `toggleFileTabsViaSettings` at `:50`; `tests/e2e/comments.spec.ts:789` (pane 300px, workspace never under it) and `:1792` (pane at the window's right edge); `tests/e2e/web.spec.ts:723` (W19, `.comments-wrap` right of the stack); `tests/e2e/shell-and-menus.spec.ts:970` (instant toggle).
